Privacy Policy

Invictus Fitness Tracker — Effective April 20, 2026

Introduction

Invictus ("the App") is a fitness and strength training tracker developed by Roy Aris. Your privacy is important. This policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is stored, and what is shared — which is nothing.

Data Collection

Invictus does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All information you enter stays entirely on your device.

The App allows you to record the following data locally:

  • Workout logs (squat, bench press, deadlift, and other exercises)
  • Body weight measurements
  • Daily calorie intake
  • Step counts
  • Personal preferences and settings

This data is stored using AsyncStorage on your device. It is never uploaded to any server, cloud service, or third-party platform by the App.

Motion & Fitness Sensors

Invictus may request access to your device's motion sensors (accelerometer/pedometer) to count your daily steps. This sensor data is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted externally.

AI Coach Feature (Optional)

The App includes an optional AI coaching feature that can generate workout summaries and guidance. To use this feature, you must provide your own Anthropic API key. When enabled, your workout data is sent directly from your device to Anthropic's API to generate responses. The developer of Invictus does not have access to your API key or the data sent in these requests. Please review Anthropic's Privacy Policy for information on how they handle API requests.

This feature is entirely optional. The App is fully functional without it.

Religious & Spiritual Content

The App displays Orthodox Christian content such as patron saints, Bible verses, and liturgical calendar information. This content is bundled within the App and does not require any network requests or personal data.

Third-Party Services

Invictus does not use:

  • Advertising frameworks or ad networks
  • Analytics or tracking tools (no Google Analytics, no Firebase Analytics, etc.)
  • Third-party login or authentication services
  • Social media SDKs
  • Any other third-party data collection services

Data Sharing

Your data is never sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party. Since no personal data leaves your device (except optionally to Anthropic's API at your explicit direction), there is nothing to share.

Data Retention & Deletion

All data is stored locally on your device. You can delete your data at any time by clearing the App's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the App. Once deleted, the data cannot be recovered, as no copies exist on any server.

Children's Privacy

Invictus is not directed at children under 13. The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
