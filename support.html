Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset all my data?

You can reset all app data using one of these methods:

  1. Open your iPhone Settings app.
  2. Scroll down and tap Invictus.
  3. Tap Clear Data (if available), or go to Settings > General > iPhone Storage > Invictus > Delete App and reinstall it.

Please note that this action is permanent. All workout logs, body weight history, and other saved data will be lost and cannot be recovered.

How do I report a bug?

Please send an email to jaykayyah@gmail.com with the following information:

  1. A brief description of the issue.
  2. Steps to reproduce the problem.
  3. Your device model (e.g., iPhone 15) and iOS version.
  4. A screenshot, if applicable.
Where is my data stored?

All your data is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. Nothing is sent to any server. If you delete the app, your data is permanently removed.

How does step counting work?

Invictus uses your device's built-in motion sensors to count steps. You will be asked to grant motion and fitness permission the first time you use this feature. All step data is processed on your device and never leaves it.

What is the AI Coach feature?

The AI Coach is an optional feature that provides workout summaries and training guidance. To use it, you need to provide your own Anthropic API key in the app settings. Your workout data is sent directly from your device to Anthropic's servers to generate responses. The developer does not have access to your API key or requests.

How do I request a new feature?

Feature requests are welcome. Send an email to jaykayyah@gmail.com with the subject line "Feature Request" and describe what you would like to see in the app.
